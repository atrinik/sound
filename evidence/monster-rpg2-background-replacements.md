Retrieved and reviewed on 2026-08-11 for Atrinik sound issue #18. This evidence
applies only to the 28 replacement source hashes below. It does not approve any
removed legacy byte stream or any other member of the source archive.

## Authoritative creator and grant

OpenGameArt user `troutsneeze` identifies Nooskewl as the user's project and
describes it as the website for their games. The same profile lists the
publication “42 Monster RPG 2 music tracks” among that user's authored uploads.
The publication identifies `troutsneeze` as its author, says the files came
from the open-sourced Nooskewl game, and exercises the game's Give It Your Own
License grant by releasing the original tracks under CC0. It explicitly
excludes `jungle_ambience` because that work is not original.

The exact retrieved evidence is preserved as Git-tracked captures:

- `evidence/captures/opengameart-troutsneeze-profile.html`, SHA-256
  `993dc4953ec925cae556d89a54c07defd8aee0ae7ff1015551efaecc8a1e493e`;
- `evidence/captures/opengameart-monster-rpg2-music.html`, SHA-256
  `80a89dc0dcb80f56b55d5904da6d37683c1222187f54dc0dbc8ae0fd6975cd77`.

The linked lossless archive is `Monster RPG 2 FLAC Music - Revised.tar.gz`,
148,110,062 bytes, SHA-256
`8d10f4b6d1526616759248aed12163e6f6f4c6781cc32be1d4cb8f42716fbf52`.
It contains 41 regular FLAC members beneath `flac-music/`; it contains no
`jungle_ambience` member. Every selected repository file is byte-identical to
the correspondingly named archive member.

CC0 permits copying, modification, format conversion, commercial use, and
redistribution without an attribution condition. Atrinik nevertheless retains
the Nooskewl and `troutsneeze` identities, source URL, original member name,
replacement mapping, and modification record.

## Decision and remaining gate

These 28 exact lossless sources have full-work conversion and redistribution
permission under `CC0-1.0`. The legacy logical keys and their runtime output
paths remain stable; the removed legacy bytes receive no approval.

Role-oriented selection used the historical notice descriptions and the
Nooskewl member names, but that is not listening approval. Every replacement
remains fail-closed on the separate source-to-Opus behavioral and critical
listening gate. That review must cover complete playback, role suitability,
duration, channels, loop boundary, perceived loudness, content, and generated
Opus behavior before publication.
